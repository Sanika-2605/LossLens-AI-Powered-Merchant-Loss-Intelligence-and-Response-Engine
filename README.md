# LossLens — AI-Powered Merchant Loss Intelligence & Response Engine

LossLens is an explainable, AI-powered merchant risk intelligence platform that helps merchants discover hidden financial loss patterns across transactions, customers, orders, refunds, and related entities.

Instead of evaluating transactions in isolation, LossLens analyzes relationships and behaviour across the merchant ecosystem to identify emerging patterns such as coordinated refund abuse, multi-account abuse, abnormal transaction clusters, and other previously unknown sources of financial exposure. It combines graph-based analysis, temporal anomaly detection, machine learning, and evidence-grounded AI investigation to determine **what is happening, why it matters, and how much money is at risk**.

For every detected pattern, LossLens constructs an evidence trail connecting the relevant customers, transactions, orders, refunds, devices, addresses, products, and timelines. It then estimates the potential financial exposure and expected loss, evaluates possible interventions, and recommends the safest action based on risk, financial impact, and potential legitimate-customer impact.

Every intervention is governed by configurable policies, confidence thresholds, escalation rules, human approval gates, and audit controls. Approved actions are executed through the connected payment infrastructure, outcomes are verified, and the complete decision lifecycle is recorded for accountability and analysis.

**Discover → Connect → Detect → Investigate → Quantify → Simulate → Govern → Act → Verify**

Unlike traditional rule-based fraud and transaction-monitoring systems that primarily ask **"Is this transaction suspicious?"**, LossLens asks a broader question:

**"Is a harmful pattern emerging across the merchant ecosystem, what evidence connects it, how much financial loss could it cause, and what is the safest response?"**

The system is specifically designed to uncover **unknown patterns**, rather than relying entirely on predefined fraud rules. This allows merchants to detect coordinated behaviour that may appear legitimate when individual transactions are examined separately.

The goal is simple: **don't just flag suspicious transactions—discover hidden loss patterns, understand their financial impact, and help merchants respond before the loss grows.**
