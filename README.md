# Recovra — Autonomous Revenue Recovery Intelligence

Recovra is an explainable, policy-constrained AI platform that helps merchants identify and recover revenue at risk of being lost.

It detects payment failures, checkout abandonment, subscription failures, mandate failures, overdue receivables, and broken promise-to-pay commitments. Recovra analyzes the cause of each revenue-loss event, predicts the probability of recovery, evaluates possible interventions, and selects the action with the highest expected recovery value.

Every recovery action is bounded by configurable policies, retry limits, contact limits, stopping rules, and human approval gates. The system executes approved workflows through payment infrastructure, verifies outcomes, and maintains a complete audit trail of every decision and action.

**Detect → Diagnose → Predict → Optimize → Govern → Execute → Verify → Learn**

Unlike traditional recovery systems that rely on fixed retry rules and generic reminders, Recovra uses a closed-loop, data-driven approach to decide not only **whether revenue is at risk**, but also **what action should be taken, when it should be taken, and when the system should stop**.

The goal is simple: **don't just detect revenue leakage—recover it and measure the actual money recovered.**
